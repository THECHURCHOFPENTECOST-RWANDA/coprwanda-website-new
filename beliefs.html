THE CHURCH OF PENTECOST INTERNATIONAL, RWANDA – OUR DOCTRINAL BELIEFS AND FUNDAMENTAL TENETS

Our faith is rooted in the Holy Scriptures and the Gospel of Jesus Christ. These eleven fundamental tenets express the doctrinal foundation of The Church of Pentecost.

Introduction

The Church of Pentecost International, Rwanda is part of The Church of Pentecost, a global Pentecostal movement rooted in classical Pentecostal theology. We uphold the authority of the Bible and the centrality of Jesus Christ, and we believe in the power and work of the Holy Spirit in the life of the Church and every believer.

The 11 Tenets of Faith

1. The Bible

We believe that the Bible is divinely inspired, infallible and inerrant in its declarations, and that it is the final authority for Christian faith and conduct.

Scripture: 2 Timothy 3:16–17.

2. The One True God

We believe in the one true God, Elohim, the Creator of the universe, who has revealed Himself as the Triune Godhead: the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit. They are one in nature, essence and attributes. God is omniscient, omnipotent and omnipresent.

Scriptures: Deuteronomy 6:4; Matthew 28:19.

3. Man’s Depraved Nature

We believe that all human beings have sinned through the fall and stand in need of repentance, regeneration and salvation through Jesus Christ.

Scriptures: Romans 3:23; Romans 5:12.

4. The Saviour

We believe that Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man. He was born of a virgin, lived a sinless life, died a vicarious and atoning death on the cross, rose bodily from the dead, ascended into heaven and will return.

Scriptures: John 1:1,14; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Acts 1:9–11.

5. Repentance, Regeneration, Justification and Sanctification

We believe that salvation comes through genuine confession and repentance of sins, justification by grace through faith in the atoning work of Jesus Christ, and ongoing sanctification by the Holy Spirit.

Scriptures: Acts 2:38; Romans 5:1; Ephesians 2:8–9; 1 Thessalonians 5:23.

6. Ordinances (Sacraments)

We recognize the following ordinances:

Water Baptism

We believe in water baptism by full immersion for believers, typically from the age of 13 and above.

Infant Dedication

We believe in presenting children to the Lord through infant dedication.

The Lord’s Supper (Communion)

We observe the Lord’s Supper regularly in remembrance of the death of Jesus Christ and as an expression of fellowship among believers.

Scriptures: Matthew 28:19; Mark 10:13–16; 1 Corinthians 11:23–26.

7. The Holy Spirit

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit, with spiritual power and gifts, and in the operation of the supernatural gifts and the fruit of the Spirit in the life of every believer.

Scriptures: Acts 2:4; 1 Corinthians 12:4–11; Galatians 5:22–23.

8. Divine Healing

We believe that divine healing is provided in the atonement of Jesus Christ. We also permit and accept medical help and treatment.

Scriptures: Isaiah 53:4–5; James 5:14–16.

9. Tithes and Offerings

We believe in supporting the work of God's Kingdom through the giving of tithes, representing one-tenth of income, and free-will offerings.

Scriptures: Malachi 3:10; 2 Corinthians 9:6–7.

10. The Second Coming and the Next Life

We believe in the physical and visible return of Jesus Christ, followed by the bodily resurrection and judgment of both the saved, who will receive eternal life, and the unsaved, who will face eternal damnation.

Scriptures: Acts 1:11; 1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; Revelation 20:11–15.

11. Marriage and Family

We believe that marriage is a sacred, lifelong union exclusively between a biological man and a biological woman.

Scripture: Genesis 2:24; Matthew 19:4–6.

Our commitment: These tenets guide our Christian faith, teaching, fellowship and service as part of The Church of Pentecost.
