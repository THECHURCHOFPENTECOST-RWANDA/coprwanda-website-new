OUR MINISTRIES

The Church of Pentecost International Rwanda serves through various ministries and directorates, each contributing to the spiritual growth, discipleship and mission of the Church.

EVANGELISM MINISTRY

The Evangelism Ministry is the evangelistic wing of the Church. It draws its membership from among Church members without restrictions on gender or age.

Its primary responsibility is to train members for soul-winning and evangelistic activities, raise new converts and prepare them for Church membership. The Ministry operates at the National, Regional/Area, District and Local Assembly levels.

Its main function is to make disciples and build up new converts until they are enabled to obey all the commandments of Jesus Christ.

WOMEN'S MINISTRY

The Women's Ministry is specifically for women in the Church. Every female member of the Church above the age of sixteen is considered a member of this Ministry.

The main objectives of the Ministry are to:

  • Encourage women to develop strong prayer lives.
  • Improve their spiritual and family lives so they can function effectively as wives and mothers.
  • Set good examples as Christian women in society.
  • Pray for the Church and its leaders.

PENTECOST MEN'S MINISTRY (PEMEM)

The Pentecost Men's Ministry (PEMEM) is a wing of The Church of Pentecost International Rwanda established to cater for the interests of all men in the Church. It provides a forum for gathering and teaching the Church's male population in all aspects of Christian life.

The Ministry organizes seminars, workshops, lectures and symposia on various aspects of life, including marriage enrichment, husbands' responsibilities in the Church and State, current national and international affairs, basic principles of domestic law and other specialized men-related topics.

CHILDREN'S MINISTRY

The Children's Ministry is devoted to meeting the spiritual, physical and emotional needs of children in the Church. Bible lessons tailored to children's needs are prepared and taught by Ministry leaders during Sunday School sessions on Sunday mornings.

Any child under the age of thirteen is welcome to be part of this Ministry.

Its main aim is to train children in the way of the Lord so that when they grow older, they will not depart from it. Our ultimate mission is to win the world for Christ through the propagation of the Gospel to the utmost parts of the earth.

CHAPLAINCY MINISTRY

In line with the Church's vision of "Possessing the Nations," The Church of Pentecost International Rwanda (COPIRWA) will make concerted efforts to promote godliness and the application of godly principles in serving all spheres of society.

Through chaplaincy, the Church seeks to provide a Christian presence in society, with chaplains serving as ambassadors of Christ and offering spiritual care. Their roles include providing support, counselling and guidance for psychosocial, spiritual and emotional needs.

Chaplains have a primary responsibility to care for patients. They may also engage with patients' families and respond to their needs as required.

YOUTH DIRECTORATE

The Youth Ministry serves the spiritual needs of individuals between the ages of thirteen and thirty-five years. The Church recognizes young people as future leaders of the Church and society. Therefore, there is a need for them to be firmly grounded in the service of the Lord.

PENTECOST STUDENTS AND ASSOCIATES (PENSA)

PENSA stands for Pentecost Students and Associates. It is the vibrant student and youth wing of The Church of Pentecost International Rwanda (COPIRWA).

PENSA brings together secondary school students, tertiary and university students, graduates and associates across various academic and professional spheres.

Its core mission focuses on dynamic evangelism, leadership training, spiritual discipleship and grooming young people to live purposeful lives and transform society for Christ.

MEDIA MINISTRY

The Media Ministry of The Church of Pentecost International Rwanda coordinates the Church's digital outreach, broadcasting and media strategy to spread the Gospel and influence all spheres of society.

It operates under the Church's strategic vision of integrating spirituality with professional work, transforming society through biblical principles and possessing the media space for Christ.

E-CHURCH

An E-Church (Electronic Church or Online Church) refers to digital, virtual or internet-based religious services, communities and platforms that allow people to participate in worship, prayer and fellowship online, either as an alternative to or in addition to physical attendance.
