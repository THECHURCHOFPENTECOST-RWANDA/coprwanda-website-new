Vision, Mission and Core Values

Our vision, mission and core values define our identity, guide our ministry and shape our commitment to transforming lives and communities through the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision Statement

To become a global Pentecostal church that is culturally relevant in vibrant evangelism, church planting, discipleship, and holistic ministry.

Our Mission Statement

To establish responsible and self-sustaining churches filled with committed, Spirit-filled Christians of character who will impact their communities.

Our Core Values

1. Evangelism

Proclaiming Jesus Christ in the power of the Holy Spirit so that individuals may trust Him as their Saviour and Lord.

2. Discipleship

Training and nurturing believers to reflect Christ-like character, holiness, righteousness and scriptural discipline.

3. Ministry of the Holy Spirit

Emphasising baptism in the Holy Spirit, divine healing, spiritual gifts, and the manifestation of the fruit of the Spirit.

4. Prayer and Devotional Life

Promoting a vibrant culture of personal and corporate prayer and devotion to God.

5. Leadership and Excellence

Developing sound, character-driven leadership and pursuing excellence in both spiritual and professional ministry.

6. Distinct Church Culture and Discipline

Upholding Christian modesty, monogamous marriage, abstinence from harmful substances, and impartial, restorative church discipline.

7. Voluntary Giving (Tithes and Offerings)

Promoting financial self-reliance through faithful, voluntary and scriptural giving without coercion.
